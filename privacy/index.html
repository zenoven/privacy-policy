Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 31, 2026

This policy explains how n8n-znv, a private workflow-automation service operated by ZenOven (“the application”), accesses, uses, stores, and shares information, including data received from Google APIs.

1. Scope and operator

This policy applies to n8n-znv and the workflows run through it. The application is a self-hosted n8n deployment intended only for users authorized by the operator. It is not offered as a public consumer service.

2. Google user data the application accesses

Depending on the workflow an authorized user runs, the application may access:

  • Google Sheets data, to read and write spreadsheet values used as workflow inputs, status fields, or results.
  • Google Drive files, to create, read, update, or organize files selected by the user or created by the workflow.
  • Google Drive file metadata, such as file name, ID, type, and location, to find and manage files required by the workflow.
  • OAuth account and token information, to maintain the authorized connection to Google APIs.

The application does not request access to Gmail, Google Contacts, Google Calendar, precise location, payment information, or advertising data as part of this Google OAuth integration.

3. How Google user data is used

Google user data is used only to:

  • perform automation explicitly configured by an authorized user;
  • read workflow inputs and write workflow results;
  • create, locate, update, and organize files required by the workflow;
  • maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and audit the workflow service; and
  • respond to an authorized user’s support or deletion request.

Google user data is not sold, rented, used for advertising, used to create advertising profiles, or used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

4. Storage and security

OAuth credentials and workflow execution data are stored within the operator’s access-controlled, self-hosted n8n environment. Data is transmitted over HTTPS when communicating with the application and Google APIs. Access is limited to the authorized user, the operator, and systems required to run the configured workflow.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. The operator uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to this limited-access service.

5. Sharing and workflow processors

The application does not disclose Google user data to third parties for advertising or sale. A configured workflow may send the minimum content necessary to a processor—such as a transcription, text-processing, or media-storage service—only when that processing is required to perform the workflow requested by the authorized user. Those providers process the data under their applicable terms and privacy commitments.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, and integrity of the application, its operator, or its users.

6. Retention and deletion

  • OAuth credentials are retained until the credential is disconnected, access is revoked, or the service is retired.
  • Workflow inputs, outputs, and execution records are retained only as needed to operate, troubleshoot, and audit configured workflows, and are deleted when no longer needed or when an authorized deletion request is completed, subject to applicable legal obligations.
  • Files and spreadsheet values stored in Google remain under the authorized user’s control and can be deleted through the relevant Google product.

7. User choices and control

An authorized user may stop future access at any time by:

  • disconnecting the Google credential in n8n;
  • revoking the application in Google Account security settings; or
  • requesting deletion of associated workflow records from the operator.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of data controlled by the operator, email zenoven@gmail.com.

8. Google API Services User Data Policy

The application’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

9. Children

The application is not directed to children and is available only to users authorized by the operator.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application’s workflows or data practices change. The current version and effective date will always be published on this page. Material changes will be communicated to authorized users when appropriate.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the operator at zenoven@gmail.com.
